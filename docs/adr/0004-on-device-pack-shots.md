# ADR 0004: On-device 3D pack shots from user-placed corners

- **Status:** Accepted, partly superseded by [ADR 0006](0006-automatic-straightening-and-catalogue-product-picture.md): the manual corner dots and the strip/bottle/tube path below are replaced; the rest still applies.
- **Date:** recorded 2026-10-22

## Context

Circulars look better with a clean 3D product shot. Image-generation AI can produce one, but it can also change the strength, logo or design on the pack. On a medicine circular the picture must be the real pack.

## Decision

- The product picture is **always built from the user's own photos and never generated by AI.**
- For a box, each labelled face (front, side, top) gets four corner dots. They are auto-suggested from a background flood-fill (`src/lib/image/corners.ts`); the user drags them, or moves a selected dot with the arrow keys (`src/components/wizard/CornerEditor.tsx`). Each face is then straightened with a homography and bilinear sampling (`src/lib/image/perspective.ts`, `src/lib/image/straighten.ts`).
- The straightened faces are wrapped onto an oblique 3D box with a soft shadow (`src/lib/image/packshot.ts`). Depth comes from the side or top photo, and a warning shows if those two disagree by more than 20%.
- The straightened faces are also what the AI reads, so small print arrives flatter and sharper.
- Bottles, tubes and strips ("Not a box") skip the corners and use background removal on the front photo instead (`src/lib/image/cutout.ts`).
- All of this runs in the browser. It costs nothing, and photos don't leave the device for this step.

## Alternatives considered

- **AI image generation or a paid background-removal service:** cost per image, risk of an altered pack, photos sent to a third party.
- **Automatic corners only:** fails on busy backgrounds; the user needs control.

## Consequences

- Quality depends on the user's photos and corner placement.
- Dragging small dots on a phone is fiddly; a magnifier is on the [roadmap](../ROADMAP.md).
- The maths is pure and unit-tested (`src/lib/__tests__/perspective.test.ts`).
